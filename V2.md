# CornMute – v2 ideas (NOT in v1, scope frozen)

- Turn individual built-in sites off.
- Auto-update of the built-in list between releases (would need care: no remote code).
- Optional "re-mute when the tab goes to the background".
- Sync "Muted sites" across devices (chrome.storage.sync) – opt-in.
- Localised listing (FR, ES, JA).
- Content-based detection: read RTA ("Restricted To Adults") labels / `rating` meta tags to catch unlisted adult sites. Needs all-sites access (host permissions + content scripts), so it changes the install prompt and privacy story.
- Merge a second open-source list (e.g. Block List Project porn list, Unlicense, ~950k entries). Skipped in v1: ~20x bigger and noisier (false positives found on university, Yahoo, Reddit-CDN and Substack subdomains).
- Let users report false positives from the popup (mailto link), feeding data/exclude.txt.

## Pricing: v1 launches free, Pro later

**v1 (now):** free, no payments, no accounts, no upsell inside the extension.

**When to add Pro:** after solid reviews (4.5★+) and roughly **500+ users**. Not before; an early paywall hurts reviews and installs.

**Rules (no bait-and-switch):**
- Every v1 feature stays free forever for everyone, existing and new users alike. Never move a free feature behind the paywall; it would tank the reviews.
- Pro = **new features only**, e.g. content-based detection (RTA labels), mute schedules, password lock for settings / "Muted sites", sync across devices.
- Price: **one-time $2.99–4.99** (no subscription for something this small).
- **Discreet billing descriptor** on card statements: e.g. "ALECBUILDS" or "ALECBUILDS CORNMUTE". Never "porn", "NSFW" or "adult". Same for receipt emails and the checkout page title.

**Payment options:**
| Option | How it works | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ExtensionPay** (extensionpay.com) | Drop-in library for Chrome extensions, Stripe underneath | Fastest to add (an hour), handles login + "has paid?" check, one-time or subscription | Uses Stripe Connect (you are the seller, so you handle sales tax/VAT yourself); small extra fee; adds a dependency |
| **Stripe** (direct) | Stripe Checkout / Payment Links + your own licence check | Lowest fees, full control, Stripe Tax available | Most work: need a small backend or licence-key system; you are the seller (tax registration and filing are on you); Stripe's adult-content rules mean the description must stay "tab muting utility" |
| **Lemon Squeezy** (or Paddle) | **Merchant of record**: they sell the product, you get paid out | They collect and remit sales tax/VAT worldwide, handle refunds and chargebacks; built-in licence keys; statement shows their name | Higher fees (~5% + 50¢); payouts are slower; review of your product category before approval |

Recommendation for a solo founder in Canada: **Lemon Squeezy (merchant of record)** with licence keys, unless ExtensionPay's speed matters more than tax handling.

**Chrome Web Store changes once it's paid:**
- Switch the developer account's **trader status** to "Trader" (EU Digital Services Act): requires showing a verified business address, phone and email on the listing.
- Update the listing: say which features are Pro, keep "v1 features are free".
- Update the privacy policy and Privacy practices tab: payment provider, licence check, what (if any) data is sent (e.g. an email address for the licence).
- Any licence check must not need new permissions beyond a single host for the licence API; justify it in the listing.
